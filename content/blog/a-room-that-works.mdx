Before choosing finishes, spend time with the plan. A room that supports movement and routine will feel more comfortable long after a trend has passed.

## Map the everyday routes

Notice how people enter, pass through, sit, work, and gather. Keep these paths clear, especially around doors, dining chairs, and frequently used storage.

## Start with the pieces that matter

Measure the bed, dining table, sofa, or work surface that the room must hold. Then plan the surrounding space around those dimensions instead of trying to fit everything at the end.

## Leave room to change

Flexible lighting, a movable side table, and storage that can adapt make a room easier to live in as needs shift. A good plan creates options without making the space feel unfinished.
