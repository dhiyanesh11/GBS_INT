The first moments of the day set a rhythm. A room can help that rhythm feel less hurried without a complete renovation.

## Give everyday things a place

Keep the objects used each morning together and easy to reach. A shallow tray, a clear shelf, or a small drawer near the entry can reduce the search for keys, glasses, or a favourite mug.

## Let daylight do its work

Where possible, keep window areas clear and use light-filtering curtains. A comfortable chair near natural light can create a quiet place to pause before the day begins.

## Make a small ritual visible

Leave a little space for the things that make a morning yours: a book, a cup, a plant, or a place to sit. Good design often comes from making useful habits feel welcome.

Start with one corner and notice what changes. A thoughtful room supports the life already happening in it.
