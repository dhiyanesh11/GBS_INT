Materials are not only a colour decision. They shape how a room feels underfoot, how it sounds, and how it will look after years of use.

## Begin with how the room is used

A busy entry needs a different floor from a quiet bedroom. Think about water, sunlight, pets, children, cleaning habits, and how much wear each surface will take.

## See samples in the actual light

Natural and artificial light change how a finish appears. Bring samples into the room and look at them at different times of day before making a final choice.

## Balance character with care

Wood, stone, cotton, and lime-based finishes bring variation and texture. Ask how each material should be cleaned and repaired, then choose the level of upkeep that suits your household.

The best palette is one that feels good at the start and still makes sense in daily life.
